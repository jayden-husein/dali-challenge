# Family Reunification

Helping people separated by a hurricane evacuation find each other, while letting anyone who doesn't want to be found stay hidden.

## People

**Registrant**:
A person recorded at a shelter by a Volunteer, with or without ID or a phone.
_Avoid_: Victim, evacuee, user, survivor

**Seeker**:
Someone looking for a Registrant, from a shelter or from their own device.
_Avoid_: Searcher, family member, requester

**Departed**:
A Registrant who has left their shelter. Undone if they come back. No longer findable, and Contact Requests for them never reach a Volunteer. The Seeker still sees "passed on", as for any other request.
_Avoid_: Discharged, checked out, inactive

**Volunteer**:
Shelter staff or a volunteer who operates the app on behalf of Registrants.
_Avoid_: Admin, operator, staff user

## Finding and contact

**Visibility**:
A Registrant's choice to be **Findable** (appears in search) or **Not findable** (never appears, as if absent). Never shown to Seekers.
_Avoid_: Privacy setting, status, listed/unlisted, ask-me-first

**Contact Request**:
A Seeker's ask to reach a Registrant. A Volunteer gets the Registrant's live consent, and the Registrant decides what the Seeker learns.
_Avoid_: Match, connection, friend request

**Request Code**:
A short readable code given to a Seeker when they send a Contact Request, used to check for a reply from any device.
_Avoid_: Ticket, tracking number, case ID

**Shelter Code**:
A short shared secret per shelter that lets a Volunteer register people at that shelter and see and answer its Contact Requests.
_Avoid_: Password, login, PIN
